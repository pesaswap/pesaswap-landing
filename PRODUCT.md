# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B decision-makers at PSPs, remittance platforms, aggregators, and regional banks evaluating payment infrastructure for East African corridors. CTOs and heads of payments comparing build-vs-buy for cross-border settlement. They arrive skeptical, technically literate, and looking for proof of live infrastructure, not promises.

## Product Purpose

Pesaswap is payment orchestration infrastructure for East Africa. It sits between platforms (Zotapay, LemFi, Nala, Seven By Far) and local payment rails (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, KCB, Co-op Bank, Choice Bank), providing smart routing, real-time FX settlement, 3-way automated reconciliation, and open PSP integration through one API. Not a PSP, not a wallet, not an app. The middleware that connects and optimises the full East African payment stack. Licensed in 4 markets (Tanzania, Uganda, Kenya, Rwanda), USD 120Mn+ processed, serving Tier-1 partners processing USD 1Bn/month.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, infrastructure-grade, African-rooted. The voice of a company that has already built the rails, acquired the licences, and is now scaling the volume. Speaks with earned confidence, not startup hype. Precise language, zero fluff. Every claim backed by live infrastructure.

## Anti-references

- Consumer fintech landing pages (Flutterwave, Paystack, PesaPal consumer-facing sites). Pesaswap is infrastructure, not a consumer product.
- Cheap young startup aesthetic: oversaturated gradients, generic stock illustrations, bubbly rounded UI, playful copy, emoji-heavy messaging. Zero tolerance for this.
- Generic SaaS templates with placeholder content and no technical depth.
- Dark-mode-for-the-sake-of-it developer tool aesthetics. The brand is rooted in African commerce, not Silicon Valley dev tools.
- Overly minimal "we just launched" single-page sites with no substance.

## Design Principles

1. **Infrastructure confidence.** The design should feel like institutional-grade software, not a pitch. Every visual element communicates "this is live, this is real, this processes money."
2. **Show the network.** Pesaswap's value is the breadth of its integrations and the depth of its rails. The design should make the network visible and tangible.
3. **Earned, not claimed.** Traction numbers, partner logos, licensing status, and live corridor data speak louder than marketing copy. Lead with proof.
4. **African-rooted, globally credible.** The design should feel like it belongs in Nairobi and London equally. Not "African-themed" decoration, but the confidence of a company built from and for the continent.
5. **Clarity over cleverness.** B2B buyers scan for relevance. Dense information presented cleanly beats sparse information presented beautifully.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Financial services context demands high contrast ratios and clear typography. Support for reduced motion preferences. No information conveyed by color alone.
